Day-by-day plan

Day 1

Arrival in Abu Dhabi

Sleep close to the airport or city and do not start driving immediately after the flight.

Day 2

Drive to Dubai

Pick up the car and drive up to Dubai. Check in, then do Downtown first because it is the cleanest first-day Dubai circuit.

Day 3

Old Dubai

Focus on the creek, abra ride, souks, and a slower cultural loop. This keeps the Dubai part from becoming too commercial.

Old Dubaiabrasouks
Day 4

Family Dubai day

Choose one child-friendly anchor: KidZania, beach, mall aquarium-type plan, or a long lunch plus hotel pool break.

family daykid-friendlylight schedule
Day 5

Dubai to Abu Dhabi

Return south with a stop only if you feel fresh. Keep the evening in Abu Dhabi quiet.

drive backbufferrest
Day 6

Louvre or beach

Back in Abu Dhabi, do the best single thing you did not fit in before: Louvre, Saadiyat, or a sea-facing walk.

Desert or Yas

Use the last open day for the strongest Abu Dhabi attraction that still feels worth it after Dubai.
