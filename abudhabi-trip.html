Trip directions

Best if Abu Dhabi is the base Lower transfer stress

Option 1 — Abu Dhabi base + desert + Louvre + Yas Island

Use Abu Dhabi as the main base. One desert evening or night safari, one Louvre Abu Dhabi day, and one Yas Island family day is the cleanest plan. Yas Island is about 30 minutes from the city centre and around 15 minutes from the airport, so it stays easy with a child.

Best if you want Dubai too Car is more practical

Option 2 — Abu Dhabi + 3 days in Dubai

Do a 3-day Dubai side trip after the Abu Dhabi days. Car rental is the most practical option if you want flexibility with luggage, stops, and timings; public transport is cheaper but much slower. A simple split: Downtown, Old Dubai, then beach/mall/family time.

Best if Elio’s pace comes first Mostly indoor + easy days

Option 3 — Elio-first family plan

Keep it slower: Louvre Abu Dhabi’s children/family programmes, KidZania or Children’s City if you do Dubai, plus one mangrove or beach morning and one desert evening. This version avoids stacking too many big-ticket days back-to-back and leaves room for rest.

How I’d shortlist the stay

If the goal is cheapest possible 4-star with a good safety margin, start with Grand Villaggio or TRYP. If you want the easiest family fit, La Quinta or Southern Sun are the stronger picks. If you prefer a beach-leaning location, Oryx is the value option to compare.
