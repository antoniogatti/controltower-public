Day 1

Arrival and easy night

Land, transfer to hotel, eat nearby, and keep the first evening simple so everyone resets after the flights.

Louvre Abu Dhabi + Saadiyat

Make this the main culture day: Louvre Abu Dhabi in the morning, lunch nearby, then a beach or promenade stop in the afternoon.

Desert day

Use the desert for the memorable big outing. Keep the morning free, then do a late-afternoon or evening safari so the heat and energy are lower.
